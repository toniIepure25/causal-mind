# CAUSAL MIND — Known vs. Unknown

An explicit boundary between what the program has established and what it has not. Part of
the `cm8-prehuman-v1.0` freeze. This is the anti-overclaiming document.

## KNOWN (established, with evidence)

- **The thought stream is forecastable (modestly).** Next-thought semantic prediction
  0.362 [0.352, 0.372], above all 8 baselines; multi-horizon prediction at h=1..10 with a
  smooth decay of the gain; TPH_semantic ≥ ~10 thoughts (~2 min, a lower bound).
  (CM-2/CM-3; C-003 L3, C-004 L5.)
- **HRF-safe fMRI adds no incremental value** for thought content in this task/window/
  horizons. (CM-5; C-005 L5 negative.)
- **The observational thought stream is not causally identifiable** (0/84 edges). (CM-6;
  C-006/C-009.)
- **The causal-inference method is validated** on an independent public dataset (ds005494):
  it correctly identifies and estimates a randomized causal effect; the specific effect is a
  valid null. (CM-7; C-010 L6.)
- **The confirmatory experiment is frozen and pre-human hardened** (10/10 gates): frozen
  reproducible forecaster (clean-room bit-identical), offline transactional engine, audited
  randomization, hardened privacy, calibrated type-I. (CM-8R; C-011 L0.)
- **The synthetic Oracle lab is ready** (4 conditions × 11 policies; RPR/PIE measured).
  (CM-9A; C-012 L0.)
- **The pre-human freeze is exact** (git `8a9d5dd`, tag `cm8-prehuman-v1.0`, all artifact
  SHAs verified).

## UNKNOWN (not established; do not claim)

- **Whether a prediction-conditioned intervention causally redirects a predicted thought in
  humans.** This is the open question the CM-8 confirmatory experiment is designed to
  answer. **No human result exists yet** (blocked on ethics + pilot).
- **The magnitude of the intervention effect** (if any). Unknown until the confirmatory run.
- **Whether the prediction generalizes** across corpora, tasks, languages, or populations.
  Established only on OSF `a56rm`.
- **The exact Thought Predictive Horizon.** Only a lower bound (≥ ~10 thoughts) is known.
- **Whether the category (non-semantic) arm is predictable.** Unvalidated.
- **Whether neural features add value under other windows/horizons/tasks.** The CM-5 null is
  conditional; other regimes are unknown.
- **The causal structure of the thought stream.** The observational data is not
  identifiable; the true causal graph is unknown.
- **When an oracle's intervention is detectable in a *real* agent.** The synthetic theory
  (CM-9) is the start; the real-agent case is unknown.
- **Any statement about free will.** The program makes **no** free-will claim (proven or
  disproven). Free will is out of scope.

## The boundary rule

- A claim may only be made at the level its evidence supports (L0–L8, see the claims
  registry). A lower-level result is never worded as a higher-level one.
- "Unknown" items are **not** turned into claims by implication, framing, or title.
- The single most important UNKNOWN (does the intervention work in humans?) is exactly what
  the frozen, ethics-gated confirmatory experiment will test — and a null there is a result.
