# CM-8 Risk Assessment

- **Overall risk level:** minimal (behavioral, no physical intervention, no clinical
  population, no deception beyond a benign fully-debriefed sham). The main risk is
  **privacy** (sensitive thought streams), addressed in the data management plan.

## Risks, mitigations, and stopping criteria

| risk | likelihood | impact | mitigation | stopping criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mild frustration from redirection tasks | moderate | low | tasks are short; participants may skip; "move away" framed as steering (not suppression, to avoid ironic rebound); breaks between blocks | participant reports sustained distress → pause, offer break, or stop |
| Discomfort from verbalizing thoughts | moderate | low | participant may type instead of speak (modality choice); may generalize or skip; never probed for sensitive topics | participant discomfort → allow skip/stop |
| Accidental disclosure of sensitive/private content | low–moderate | moderate–high (privacy) | task does not elicit sensitive topics; skip/"pass" allowed; raw text encrypted + access-limited; deletion policy; breach procedure (DMP §11) | distressing disclosure → pause, support, do not press; breach → notify per GDPR, pause study |
| Perceived evaluation of mental ability | low | low | explicit debrief that there is no right/wrong and no scoring of "good" thinking; no performance feedback during the task | participant feels judged → reassure, reframe, offer stop |
| Concern about being "predicted" | low–moderate | low–moderate (trust) | lay materials clearly state the program analyzes only the broad topic of reported words, does not read the brain, does not predict the future/destiny, and does not control the mind; no exact prediction shown (pre-Oracle) | participant unease → explain, offer stop |
| Fatigue (24 trials) | moderate | low | ~45–60 min session; breaks; pilot measures fatigue and can reduce trial count (re-powered) before confirmatory | fatigue impairs data quality → shorten session / add breaks (amendment) |
| Demand characteristics / strategy adaptation | moderate | low (validity, not safety) | sham + counterbalancing + blinding; no more than 2 consecutive same-condition trials; pilot checks for strategy adaptation | n/a (validity, handled in analysis) |

## Non-goals (what the study will NOT do)

- Will NOT induce participants to disclose information they do not wish to disclose (no
  probing of sensitive topics; skip/generalize always allowed).
- Will NOT read thoughts from the brain or claim to predict the future.
- Will NOT reveal the model's exact next-thought prediction (pre-Oracle).
- Will NOT use aversive, personal, or evaluative content.

## Allow skipping / withholding

Participants may skip any round, say "pass," or describe a thought in general terms, where
compatible with the protocol. Skipping is logged but never penalized.

## Adverse-event / withdrawal procedure

- Any adverse event or distress → immediate pause; participant support; resume only with
  supervisor (+ ethics if needed) sign-off.
- Voluntary withdrawal at any time; data before withdrawal retained unless deletion
  requested within the window (DMP §5).
