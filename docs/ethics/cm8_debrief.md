# CM-8 Debrief Script

Delivered at the end of the session, after all trials.

## 1. Purpose

"Thank you for taking part. The purpose of this study was to understand how a person's
stream of thoughts changes over time, and whether a brief instruction can change the
direction of those thoughts. This is basic cognitive-science research."

## 2. The four conditions (including the sham)

"You were randomly assigned, round by round, to one of four conditions:
- **Control:** you were asked to keep thinking naturally.
- **Sham (placebo):** you were given a neutral instruction with no intended effect — it was
  a 'placebo' to make sure any effect was due to the redirection content, not just to being
  given an instruction.
- **General redirect:** you were asked to deliberately move your thinking away from what you
  had been thinking about.
- **Specific cue:** you were shown a single word to think about.
The order was randomized and balanced, and you were not told which condition you were in at
any given round."

## 3. What the program did (and did not do)

"A program analyzed the **broad topic** of the words you reported and estimated the general
direction your reported thoughts were likely to continue in. It **did not read your private
thoughts from your brain**, did not know what you really meant, did not predict your future,
and did not control your mind. You were always in control of what you said or typed."

## 4. No score, no judgment

"There was no right or wrong way to think, and your thinking was not scored or judged. The
study looks at general patterns across many people, not at your individual performance."

## 5. Data and privacy

"Your reports are stored under a study code, not your name. We mainly use the meaning of your
reports; raw text is stored encrypted, access-limited, and deleted after the study (or kept
longer only if you agreed separately). Your results may appear in publications, but never in
a way that identifies you."

## 6. Questions + data deletion

"Do you have any questions? If you would like your raw text destroyed now, or if you have any
concern, please tell me and I will note it / contact the data-protection officer."
