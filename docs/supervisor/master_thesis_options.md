# CAUSAL MIND — Master's Thesis Options

Four viable Master's thesis directions, all grounded in the completed, pushed, pre-human
work. Each lists scope, what it delivers, feasibility, and risk. **Option B is defensible
today** (no new human data needed); **Option A is the north star** (the first causal test
of voluntary redirection of a predicted thought) but is ethics-gated.

---

## Option A — The confirmatory Break-the-Chain experiment (CM-8) as the thesis

- **Scope.** Run the frozen CM-8 confirmatory experiment: within-subject, randomized,
  4-condition (CONTROL/SHAM/GENERAL-REDIRECT/SPECIFIC-CUE), N=20, 24 trials, primary
  outcome BRP, subject-clustered permutation test.
- **Deliverable.** The first experiment to test **voluntary redirection of a predicted
  thought** — a gap no public dataset fills. A positive or a null is a result; the method
  is already validated (CM-7).
- **Feasibility.** The infrastructure is ready (10/10 pre-human gates, offline engine,
  hardened privacy, audited randomization). **Blocked on:** supervisor sign-off → ethics
  approval (submission deadline **5 Oct 2026** for the **5 Nov 2026** meeting) → a small
  gated pilot (CM-8P) → the confirmatory run (CM-8H).
- **Risk.** Ethics timing is the critical path; N=20 is under-powered for small effects
  (documented); a null is a legitimate outcome. The frozen protocol is **not** to be
  changed to chase a positive.
- **Best if** the goal is the strongest, most novel contribution and the ethics timeline
  holds.

## Option B — Forecast → Explain → Intervene (method) as a self-contained thesis

- **Scope.** The completed program with **no new human data**: CM-2/CM-3 (prediction,
  L3/L5), CM-5 (neural null), CM-6 (0/84 identifiable), CM-7 (causal-method validation on
  ds005494, L6). The capstone is the **validated causal-inference method**, not a new
  human experiment.
- **Deliverable.** A complete, reproducible thesis: a modest but real prediction result,
  two clean negative results reported honestly, and a causal method validated on an
  independent public dataset. No ethics dependency.
- **Feasibility.** **Ready now** — all results are done, pushed, and red-teamed. Can be
  written and defended without waiting for ethics or a pilot.
- **Risk.** Less "new experiment" than Option A; the prediction effects are modest and the
  test set is small (n=17). Mitigated by the breadth (forecast + explain + intervene) and
  the honest treatment of the negatives.
- **Best if** a defensible, complete thesis is needed on a fixed timeline, or if the
  ethics timeline is uncertain.

## Option C — The synthetic Oracle theory (CM-9A → CM-9) as the thesis

- **Scope.** Develop the synthetic Oracle lab into a **theory of prediction vs.
  intervention**: the Relative Prediction Ratio (RPR) and Prediction–Intervention Effect
  (PIE) across 4 oracle conditions (HIDDEN/REVEAL/VETO/REDIRECT) and 11 agent policies,
  plus the computational-irreducibility (O10) and game-theoretic best-response (O7) probes.
- **Deliverable.** A theoretical/synthetic contribution characterizing **when an oracle's
  intervention is detectable** in the agent's self-prediction. No human data, no ethics.
- **Feasibility.** Synthetic (no ethics, no pilot). Extensible; the lab is already
  `CM9A_SYNTHETIC_ORACLE_READY`.
- **Risk.** It is a synthetic exploration (no claim about real human thought); it needs a
  strong theoretical framing and a clear connection to the empirical program to stand as
  a thesis on its own.
- **Best if** the interest is theoretical (prediction/intervention, free will as a
  formal object) and a synthetic contribution is acceptable.

## Option D — The full program as one thesis (CM-8 as capstone if allowed)

- **Scope.** The whole program as a single thesis: forecast (CM-2/3), explain (CM-5/6),
  intervene (CM-7 method + CM-8 confirmatory as the capstone). If ethics does not clear in
  time, the capstone falls back to the CM-7 method validation (Option B's capstone).
- **Deliverable.** The most comprehensive account of the program, with the causal
  experiment as the climax when (and only when) it is run.
- **Feasibility.** Largest scope. The CM-8 capstone is ethics-gated; the rest is done.
- **Risk.** Scope creep and timeline risk; the capstone may not be runnable before the
  defense. Mitigated by the explicit fallback to Option B's capstone.
- **Best if** the supervisor wants the full arc and the ethics timeline is favorable.

---

## Recommendation

- **Primary:** **Option A** (the north star) **with Option B as the guaranteed floor.**
  Start the ethics submission immediately (deadline 5 Oct 2026). If ethics clears and the
  pilot passes, the thesis is Option A. If not, the thesis is Option B (complete and
  defensible today).
- **Secondary:** **Option C** as a standalone theoretical thesis or as a chapter within
  Option A/B (the synthetic Oracle lab is a natural "theory" chapter).

## What is common to all options

- The pre-human freeze `cm8-prehuman-v1.0` and its hashes.
- The claim-level evidence matrix (`docs/claims/evidence_matrix.md`).
- The honest treatment of every negative result.
- No free-will claim; no overclaiming; no change to the frozen protocol.
