# Smoke: Researcher Literature Scan

Task SMOKE-RESEARCHER-001. Three closest public research threads to
"predicting the next spontaneous thought from fMRI plus transcript."

## 1. Mind-wandering prediction
Establishes that spontaneous thought / mind-wandering episodes are tied to the
default mode network and can be detected from neural signals (fMRI/EEG) — e.g.,
Christoff et al. 2009 (PNAS) and the broader DMN literature. Main limitation for
our goal: these studies predict the *occurrence* or *category* of mind-wandering
(is one mind-wandering? what temporal orientation?), not the *specific semantic
content* of the next thought.

## 2. Semantic decoding of naturalistic fMRI
Establishes that fMRI activity during naturalistic stimuli (speech, video) can be
decoded into semantic / linguistic representations — e.g., Huth et al. 2016
(Natural Scenes Dataset), Nishimoto et al. 2011, Kay et al. 2020. Main limitation:
these are *reconstructive* (current stimulus from current brain state), not
*prospective* (next thought from current state), and are driven by external
stimuli rather than internally generated spontaneous thought.

## 3. Thought dynamics / sequence modeling
Establishes that spontaneous thought has statistical structure and can be sampled
and modeled as a sequence / dynamical system — e.g., thought sampling (Klinger &
Cox 1979), Singer 1980, and mind-wandering dynamics work. Main limitation: these
are descriptive / statistical (characterizing the distribution and transitions of
thoughts), not a *neural* forecast of the specific next thought's content.
